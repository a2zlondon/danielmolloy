If a client is acquiring a software, SaaS or AI business, legal due diligence is essential — but it does not answer every technical question.

Contracts can confirm IP ownership.
They cannot tell you whether the codebase is maintainable.

Data clauses can identify legal exposure.
They cannot tell you whether the cloud architecture is scalable.

An AI clause can describe a product claim.
It cannot tell you whether the AI is real, defensible, or just a thin wrapper around commodity tooling.

That is where technical due diligence should sit alongside legal due diligence.

• source code and architecture
• cloud cost and scalability
• security and deployment maturity
• technical debt
• team dependency and bus factor
• AI capability claims

The output is written for transaction teams: risk ratings, commercial implications, remediation actions, questions for management and post-close priorities.
