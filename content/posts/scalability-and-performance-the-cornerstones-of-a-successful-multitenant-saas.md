Scalability and performance are two essential characteristics of a successful multitenant SaaS application. As your user base grows, your application must be able to handle increasing workloads without sacrificing performance.

**Cloud infrastructure** is a popular choice for building scalable SaaS applications. Cloud providers offer a wide range of services and resources that can be easily scaled up or down as needed.

**Microservices architecture** is another effective approach to building scalable applications. This architecture breaks down the application into smaller, independent services that can be scaled individually.

When selecting your tech stack, consider the following factors:

-   **Programming languages:** Choose languages that are well-suited for building scalable and performant applications, such as Python, Ruby, or Node.js.
-   **Databases:** Select a database that can handle large datasets and scale efficiently, such as PostgreSQL or MongoDB.
-   **Frameworks:** Consider using frameworks that simplify development and improve performance, such as Django, Rails, or Express.

By carefully selecting your tech stack, you can ensure that your SaaS application is scalable and performs well, even as your user base grows.
